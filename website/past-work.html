Fusion 360 Intro Exercises

The phone stand and a name tag were class assignments to get used to Fusion 360.

What I learned

  • Moving the camera, extruding, and extruding into an object to cut it away
  • Fillets, and finding the text tool, which showed me how much more Fusion can do
  • Sketching was the hardest. I come from Blender, so I kept trying to scale and extrude like a plane. Now it feels easy.

Name tag

The first version was a plain extruded name. Then I tried a Chinese style pattern, but I got lost following a tutorial and my files got messed up, so I have no screenshots. A classmate liked the idea and had it made for them, so I switched to a "poke through" name where the letters are cut through the tag.

After printing I saw a problem: letters like A leave a small floating triangle in the middle with nothing holding it in.

What I will do differently

Think about the weak points of the 3D printer while designing, not after. I will do this for the chess board hinge so it does not fail or stick.

Name Tag

My first Fusion 360 exercise. It taught me the basics: moving the camera, extrude (including extruding into a shape to cut it), fillets, and searching for tools. Sketch was the hardest part for me because I came from Blender and kept trying to use it like a plane.

  1. Extruded name. Printed fine.
  2. A Chinese-style pattern. I followed a tutorial but the files got messed up, so I dropped it.
  3. Poke-through letters, cut all the way through the tag. This was the final one.

After printing I noticed the small triangle inside the A fell out, because it wasn't connected to anything. Lesson: think about what the printer can't do before printing. I need to remember this for the hinge on my chess board.
