# Design Brief: Chinese Class Frame

## Project Overview
It's a frame for a poster we made in Chinese class. The poster represents our class, each of us, and it's a present for our Chinese teacher because it's our last class with her and we're not learning with her anymore.

Me and Dennis made the frame. Dennis helped very much to sand it.

### What the poster means
- The top line is Ethan, showing how he gets the highest grades and is generally better than us
- The box is the class
- The line in the middle is me, because I range: sometimes really good (high) and sometimes bad (low)
- The curved line on my line represents Maymoe, because sometimes she's better or sometimes worse, hence the slant
- The 口 (kou) is Kai, because he's buns at Chinese. There's a pit under him with fire, showing he is hung by us and there's always a threat under him, though he could never fail because our teacher had a no fail policy where he would retake till he passed

## Target Audience
Our Chinese teacher, Wu Laoshi.

She has a spot on the wall she designated for us, and it will have a plaque: "Class of 2030". I'll try making the plaque in DT.

## Objectives
- Make the poster in Chinese class (outside of DT)
- Get the frame measured
- Cut it out
- Glue it
- Sand it (took very long because we wanted high quality)
- Oil it (tomorrow)
- Make the plaque

## Scope of Work
- Frame: measure, cut, glue and nail, sand, oil
- Fit the poster and acrylic
- Plaque for the wall spot

## Constraints
- A lot of time, because it's a present to her
- Not much else

## Design Requirements
- Size: just measured our dimensions of our poster with an inch margin out. Exact numbers TBD
- Joints: glue and some wood nailed together. It looks like those large structural beams in construction, with a plate connection together on the corners, and 2 in the middle on both sides of the long edge to keep the paper in
- Holding the poster: tape and the plate for the calligraphy
- Acrylic: acts as the glass to protect the image. It's fitted in and taped too so it can't get out
