Past Work

3D prints made before this project started, kept here as a record of earlier practice. The phone stand and the name tag were intro exercises to get used to Fusion 360.

Phone Stand

An intro exercise to practise Fusion 360, made by following a tutorial. It used the same tools as the name tag and printed perfectly the first time.

Name Tag

My first Fusion 360 exercise. It taught me the basics: moving the camera, extrude (including extruding into a shape to cut it), fillets, and searching for tools. Sketch was the hardest part for me because I came from Blender and kept trying to use it like a plane.

  1. Extruded name. Printed fine.
  2. A Chinese-style pattern. I followed a tutorial but the files got messed up, so I dropped it.
  3. Poke-through letters, cut all the way through the tag. This was the final one.

After printing I noticed the small triangle inside the A fell out, because it wasn't connected to anything. Lesson: think about what the printer can't do before printing. I need to remember this for the hinge on my chess board.
