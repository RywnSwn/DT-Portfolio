# Design Story (design cycle writeup)

My answers to the design cycle questions for the Book Chess Board. Filled in as the project goes.

## 1. The problem / where the idea came from
- I love chess
- Saw a TikTok where people made board games that fold up into books, so they look good on a shelf
- I couldn't make one as sophisticated as theirs, so I simplified it

## 2. First idea and why it changed
- First idea: covers that open like a real book, with the board inside
- Too advanced: it needed hinges for the cover AND hinges for the board, plus a way to stop the board folding too far so it stays rigid and flat
- It was too hard to picture in my head, and too hard to picture how it would print

## 3. Why this approach
- New version: a static hardcover case, laser cut wood. The board still folds and slides into the cover
- It stays faithful to the more advanced version
- I like the idea of a board that folds into a book
